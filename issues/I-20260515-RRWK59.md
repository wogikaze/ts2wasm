## Notes

### 2026-05-15 creation evidence

- From `artifacts/coverage/results/test262-summary.json`:

  - BigInt requires native i64 support in both IR and WASM backend

- **Broken**: `<<` and `>>` shift operators with non-BigInt shift amount (TypeError: Cannot mix BigInt and other types); BigInt64Array/BigUint64Array element storage (same bug as all TypedArrays — tracked in J2X65E)
- Verified via test cases on iwasm
