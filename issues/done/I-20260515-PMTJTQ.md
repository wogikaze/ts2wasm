## Notes

### 2026-05-15 creation evidence

- Exposed after `I-20260515-4AWCN5` rewrote the test262 `deepEqual.js` comparator to avoid the `compareEquality` IIFE helper blocker.
- Direct diagnostic: `expected Semicolon, got Some(Comma)`.

### 2026-05-16 implementation

Added comma expression statement parsing:

1. **`crates/syntax/src/ast.rs`**: Added `Expr::Sequence { exprs: Vec<Expr>, span: Span }` variant to `Expr` enum.
2. **`crates/frontend/src/parser/statements_general.rs`**: Modified `expression_statement()` to detect comma after the first expression and build a `Sequence`.
3. **`crates/ir/src/builtin_resolved.rs`**: Added `ResolvedExpr::Sequence(Vec<ResolvedExpr>)` variant.
4. **`crates/ir/src/builtin_resolver.rs`**: Added Sequence handling in `resolve_expr()` and `fold_expr()`.
5. **`crates/ir/src/lowered/resolver/expr/mod.rs`**: Lower `ResolvedExpr::Sequence` to `LoweredExpr::Block` with each sub-expression as `LoweredStmt::Expr` (except the last, which is the result).
6. **All downstream match arms**: Added `Expr::Sequence` and `ResolvedExpr::Sequence` handling in 14 match expressions across 9 files.

### Design decisions

- Comma operator is handled at the expression-statement level, not in the general `expression()` function, because `expression()` is also used in argument/array contexts where commas are separators (not the comma operator).
- At the IR level, `a, b, c` lowers to `LoweredExpr::Block { stmts: [Expr(a), Expr(b)], result: c }`.
