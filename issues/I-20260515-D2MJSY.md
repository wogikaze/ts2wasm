## Notes

### 2026-05-16 close — currently not cost-effective

Persistent runner approach was evaluated and is not currently cost-effective.

Observed:
- wasmtime-based runner is slower than iwasm CLI for tiny test262 wasm cases (763ms vs 585ms avg — 1.3x).
- iwasm execution lower bound remains too high for full semantic-fast under 300s.
- Estimated full semantic-fast lower bound with ANY runtime:
  - 24K runnable files × ~0.5s / 24 workers = ~500s
  - plus wasm emit / scheduling / JSONL overhead
- Process-spawn removal alone is insufficient to reach 300s.

Decision:
- Do not continue as P1 optimization.
- Full semantic-fast becomes CI/nightly or manual heavy mode.
- Default developer loop uses build-only plus optional sampled semantic.
